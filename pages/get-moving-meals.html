Get Moving Meals

A production meal planning platform serving 84+ users. Developed in close collaboration with the client to deliver automated Stripe billing, medication-safe filtering, and one-tap meal swaps.

Overview

Get Moving Meals is designed to simplify meal planning and nutrition tracking. Built from the ground up as a dedicated client solution, it employs a clean architecture to ensure a highly maintainable and adaptable codebase. The platform currently has generated over 168 targeted meal plans for users with specific dietary requirements.

Repwise

Repwise Fitness helps you plan, track, and improve your workouts with a clean strength training experience.

Overview

Build a plan around your goals, equipment, and schedule, then follow it session by session with rest timers, exercise guidance, plate calculator support, and workout history. Repwise also uses recovery and readiness signals to help you understand when to push, when to recover, and how your training is trending over time.

Repwise Fitness is designed for lifters who want a focused, simple, and premium workout companion without clutter.

Features

  • → Personalized onboarding for goals, training location, equipment, and schedule
  • → Workout plans for gym, home gym, dumbbells, or bodyweight training
  • → Active session tracking with rest timers and exercise details
  • → Plate calculator for barbell work
  • → Muscle readiness insights based on recent training
  • → Workout history and progress tracking
  • → Optional health context for smarter recovery signals
  • → Guest preview mode for trying the app before creating an account

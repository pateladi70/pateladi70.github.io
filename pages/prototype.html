Tasks

  1. 1. Click Login to sign in.
  2. 2. You'll land on the Dashboard.
  3. 3. Click Start Morning Review to begin.
  4. 4. You'll be taken to the Review Screen.
  5. 5. Click the Risk button.
  6. 6. You'll see the Risk Detail screen.
  7. 7. Click Drill to send a drill request.
  8. 8. Review employee completed & uncompleted tasks.
  9. 9. Click Send Reminder to email the employee.
  10. 10. Send the request email to notify of the re-assigned task.
  11. 11. You’ll see a Confirmation screen.
  12. 12. Click Log Out to return to the login screen.
